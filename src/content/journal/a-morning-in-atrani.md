At seven, the piazza in Atrani belongs to the man who sweeps it, two cats, and the woman who opens the bar. Fishing boats sit on the pebbles under the arches of the coast road, and the bells of San Salvatore start the day a little earlier than you would like.

It is a five-minute walk from Amalfi through a tunnel, but the change is complete. There are no coaches here, no shops selling limoncello in novelty bottles. Just a fountain, a church, a handful of restaurants, and stairs: hundreds of them, climbing between the houses.

## Climb before breakfast

Take the steps behind the piazza and keep going until the town is a fan of roofs below you. The light at this hour is soft and sideways, catching the lemon terraces across the valley.

## Swim from the town beach

The little beach under the road fills by eleven. Before then you can swim out past the boats and look back at a town that hardly seems to have changed in a century.

## Then leave it to the day

By late morning Atrani gets busier, and that is the moment to take the coast bus somewhere else, coming back at dusk when the piazza fills with families and the restaurant tables spill across the stones.
