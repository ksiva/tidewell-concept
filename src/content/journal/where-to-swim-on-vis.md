Vis spent decades as a military island, closed to foreign visitors until 1989. The result is a coast with very little built on it, and coves that still feel found rather than marketed.

## The south side, by bike

Rent a bike in town and ride the old road south. The climb is honest, the descent is the reward, and the coves on this side face open sea with water clear enough to see your shadow on the bottom.

## The islets, by boat

A day with a local skipper takes you to the small islands off the coast, with lunch at a family konoba that only opens when the boats come in. Book the day before; the good skippers fill up.

> Coves that still feel found rather than marketed.

## The town, at dusk

Back in town, swim from the rocks along the promenade as the light goes. Then sit outside with a glass of local white and watch the ferry come in from Split, bringing the next people who are about to discover how slow an island can be.
